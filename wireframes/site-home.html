Lit like a product shoot, not a filter.

Most mockup tools paste a screenshot into a flat frame. Shotlight builds a scene with real lights and renders it like a photograph.

  • Real lights

    Softbox, octabox, window or sun. Shadows soften with distance and sharpen at the contact point, like on set.

  • Path-traced photo quality

    Reflections, contact shadows and glass, denoised in the browser. Fast preview while you work, photo quality when you export.

  • A real lens

    Focal length, aperture and focus; bokeh where you want it, sharp where it matters.

  • Generic devices

    No brand logos, no licensing worries. Bring your own frame when you need a specific one.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes, for the whole beta. Everything you make stays yours, and we announce prices before anything changes.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Shotlight runs in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on any computer with WebGL 2.

What happens to my screenshots?

They are stored in your account, in the EU, and nowhere else. Download everything or delete the account from the menu at any time.

Can I use the output commercially?

Yes, for anything: your website, store listings, ads, pitch decks. The devices are generic, so there is no brand licensing to worry about.

Will it run on my laptop?

The fast preview runs on integrated GPUs; it lowers its resolution while you drag if it has to. Photo quality takes longer on slower machines, but it finishes.
